Intro

The mission statement of Oxxy from what I perceive is to adulate and pedestal different forums of art. Oxxy aims to be the “tailor” that perfectly “hem” the “suit” (suit being art) to said artist’s needs.

What I aim to do with this exhibition with the space and manpower the team provides is helping make visuals for an online collective named Tomodachi.

Why Tomo?

This is an online group that has a distinct, unique sound that cannot be quantified very easily. Personally when I listen to their beats, I feel motivated to try harder. They’re usually upbeat with the drums, but sometimes it can deviate into a more smooth atmosphere. But overall, the irregular drum patterns give their sound a very hectic vibe and I personally love it.

They currently use the design font of [Nintendo Wii], so the idea I had was to make an interactive visual where I would have a wide array of people react to their sounds which are already left field— but the main goal of the exhibition is to get people to experience something new but also to capture the vast range of human emotions.

While someone like me is already in the Rabbit Hole, the average student in Boston University is not someone who would listen to this kind of genre. But also, you find a couple of people who do actually listen to this music, and overall I just want to see how people react to new ideas. How does one react to change?

New ideas when presented to the general public tend to be met with ambivalent reactions because the zeitgeist at times does not know how to interpret the grey zones. They like things to be white and black. It’s either I like it or I don’t for the collective. But when you present novel things and experiences to university students, I really want to aim to capture the emotions that go behind said people.

I think that humans as a whole are very unique. We are not beings that can be categorized and quantified easily. Learning how humans live is almost like a never ending videogame. It’s just so intriguing to see how people act. Sure, we can be evil. But we can also be altruistic. We can be lazy and motivated. This consistent contradiction of our being just makes us humans. And because of this, the essence of humanity for me is a charcuterie board of cheeses and meats that never disappoint, and I kind of want to capture that said amorphous feeling.

Purpose

I think this project does three things. It first puts a highly pro-social narrative onto art. The artists are collaborating with each other to then let other humans react to art made by humans:

Oxxy is helping Words with a vision that includes Tomodachi.

But also, this project inherently kind of captures the telos or core values of every group:

Oxxy wants to pedestal creators and artists. Words main tenant is to build good community. And Tomodachi’s motto is “Let’s all be friends”.

And I personally think that Tomodachi deserves more recognition which is why I want to work on visuals for the group. And with said extra manpower, I think I can use Oxxy as a catalyst to help make this vision I have greater.
